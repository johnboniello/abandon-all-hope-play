Playing on an iPhone or iPad

  • Turn the phone sideways.
  • For full screen: open a game, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app.
  • The first load downloads about 10 MB, so use Wi-Fi.
  • Sound starts after your first tap. Check the silent switch.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser on this device. Clearing Safari's website data erases it.

How to play

  • Tap to walk, talk and use things. Press and hold to look.
  • Tap an item in the bar, then tap what to use it on.
  • Hold ? to show everything you can tap.
